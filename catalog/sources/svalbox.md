UNIS's systematic digitization of Svalbard field geology — an interactive map of digital
outcrop models (photogrammetry/lidar) built to support "sustainable, safe and quantitative"
Arctic fieldwork. Free and unrestricted access with direct download links per site; growing
collection rather than a finished archive, so site coverage is uneven across the archipelago.
Run by the Department of Arctic Geology (PM: Kim Senger).
